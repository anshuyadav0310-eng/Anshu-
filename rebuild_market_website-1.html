How ReBuild Market works

A simple way to connect surplus materials with people who can use them.

1

Seller lists

Add the material name, quantity, condition, location and asking price with clear photos.

2

Buyer discovers

Search listings and compare material details, prices and pickup locations.

3

Arrange collection

Buyer and seller discuss the details and agree on pickup or delivery independently.

For sellers

Have extra materials?

List usable surplus materials and help someone else complete a project. Be honest about condition, quantity and age.

Safety first: Do not list hazardous, damaged or safety-critical materials unless they have been properly checked and certified.
